# Deadlight Greenlight
Similar to the Boss Rush mod but instead of having to do the Missile Silos step it takes you straight to the fight!
With a vote to start system and a toggle for other enemies!

# Settings
## General
### Grace Period before the game starts - Default: 5 Seconds
How long it takes for the mode to actually start.
### Initial Green Light Length (in seconds) - Default: 90 Seconds
How long the first Green Light will last.
### Do Point Drain - Default: True
Whether to make the player lose points when they move on red light. This triggers before the player loses health.
### Point Drain Amount - Default: 100
How much points that the player will lose per second.
### Do Health Drain - Default: True
Whether to damage the player when they move on red light. This will only occur if Point Loss is disabled or the players points reached zero.
### Remove Round Delay - Default: False
Remove the delay in between rounds.
### Damage Drain Amount - Default: 15
How much damage that the player will take per second.
### Enable the Child Laugh sound - Default: True
Toggle the Child Laugh sound when players are caught moving.
## Punishments
### Critical Health - Default: True
Players health will be forced to low health.
### Strip Ammo - Default: True
The ammo of the players current weapon will be stripped.
### Deplete Armor - Default: True
Armor will be depleted instantly.
### Blurry Vision - Default: True
Your vision is pretty blurry.
### Random Teleport - Default: True
You randomly get teleported.
### Lose Random Perk - Default: True
You randomly lose a perk.
